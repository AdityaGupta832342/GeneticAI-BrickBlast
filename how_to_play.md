How do I play?
🎯 Your Goal
Break all the bricks and keep the board clear. Plan each shot carefully to destroy as many bricks as possible before they reach the bottom line.
 
🌀 Aiming and Shooting
Swipe to aim, then release to shoot. Balls bounce off walls and smash through multiple bricks. The better your angle, the more bricks you can break in one shot!
 
💡 Planning Your Shots
Each brick displays a number indicating how many hits it needs to break. Aim for clusters or corners to create long bounce chains that deal maximum damage. Prioritize high-value bricks or rows close to the bottom.
 
💥 Power Blocks
As you play, you’ll encounter special Power Blocks that boost your shots and create powerful effects.
 
🔁 After Your Shot
When the last ball touches the bottom edge, all remaining bricks shift down one row and a new round begins. You can recall all balls to the starting position at any time by tapping the button below the board.
 
🧠 Pro Tips
Use walls to your advantage for better angles.
 
Try to trigger Power Blocks together for bigger combos.
 
Stay calm and precise — the game rewards smart planning, not speed.
